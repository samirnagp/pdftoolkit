About Excel to PDF

Sharing a spreadsheet as a PDF keeps your numbers and layout fixed, so nobody changes a formula by accident. It is handy for price lists, timetables, marksheets and reports.

Tips for better results

  • Set the print area and page orientation in Excel before converting. The PDF follows those settings.
  • Wide sheets can split across pages, so use landscape or fit-to-width in Excel.
  • Hide or remove sheets you don’t want in the PDF.

Excel to PDF: frequently asked questions

Why does my table split over several pages?

The sheet is wider than one page. Set landscape or fit-to-width in Excel, then convert again.

Are formulas kept in the PDF?

No. A PDF shows the results, not the formulas.

Can I convert one sheet only?

Save a workbook with just that sheet, then convert it.

Is there a size limit?

Files up to 25 MB are accepted.
