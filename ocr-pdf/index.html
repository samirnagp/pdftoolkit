OCR PDF: Make Scans Searchable

A scanned PDF is just pictures of pages. OCR reads the text in those pictures so you can search, select and copy it.

    How to use OCR PDF

    1. Choose your scanned PDF.
    2. Select the language: English, Hindi, or both.
    3. Click Convert and download the searchable PDF.

    Why use EasyPDF

    Searchable text

    Find words, copy paragraphs and use the file with other tools.

    English and Hindi

    Pick one language or both for mixed documents.

    Skips pages with text

    Pages that already have text are left as they are.

    Good to know

    • OCR reads page by page, so long scans take several minutes. Try a few pages first.
    • Clear, straight scans at 200 to 300 dpi give the best results.
    • After OCR, you can convert the result to Word with PDF to Word. Files up to 25 MB are accepted.

    Questions about OCR PDF

    How do I know if my PDF is scanned?

    Try selecting text with your mouse. If you can’t, it is probably a scan.

    Will the file look different afterwards?

    No. The pages look the same, with an invisible text layer added.

    How accurate is OCR?

    It depends on the scan. Clean, well-lit, straight pages work best. Always check important text.
