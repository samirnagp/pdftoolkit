About OCR PDF

A scanned PDF is just a picture of text. OCR (optical character recognition) reads that picture and adds real text behind it, so you can search, select and copy words.

Tips for better results

  • Start with a straight, sharp scan. Blurry or tilted pages reduce accuracy.
  • Use Rotate PDF first if pages are sideways.
  • After OCR, convert to Word with PDF to Word if you want to edit.

Privacy

This tool uses our server. Your file is sent over an encrypted connection and deleted right after the result is sent. See the Privacy Policy.

OCR PDF: frequently asked questions

What does OCR do?

It turns an image of text into searchable and copyable text.

Why are some words wrong?

Poor scans, small print and handwriting reduce accuracy. Rescan at higher quality if you can.

Does it change how the pages look?

The pages keep their appearance. Text is added behind the image.

What is the size limit?

Files up to 25 MB are accepted.
