About Rotate PDF

Sideways or upside-down pages are common in scanned files. Rotate the whole document and save it in the right orientation, so it opens correctly next time.

Tips for better results

  • Choose 90° to turn clockwise, 180° to flip upside down, or 270° to turn counter-clockwise.
  • Rotation applies to all pages. For only some pages, extract them with Extract Pages first.
  • Rotate before OCR for best text recognition.

Privacy

This tool runs in your browser, so your file is never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Rotate PDF: frequently asked questions

Does it rotate every page?

Yes, all pages are rotated by the angle you choose.

Is my file uploaded?

No. It runs in your browser.

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. It only changes the page orientation.

Can I rotate just one page?

Extract that page, rotate it, then merge it back.
