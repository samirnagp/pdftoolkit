About Scan to PDF

No scanner? Photograph your pages with your phone and turn them into a neat PDF. Everything runs on your device, so private papers such as IDs and bank letters are not sent to a server.

Tips for better results

  • Place the page on a flat, dark surface with even light, and hold the phone directly above it.
  • Take one photo per page and fill the frame to avoid wasted margins.
  • Make the text searchable afterwards with OCR PDF.

Privacy

This tool runs in your browser, so your file is never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Scan to PDF: frequently asked questions

Is the document sent to your server?

No. This tool works inside your browser.

How do I get a sharper scan?

Use good light, avoid shadows and keep the phone steady.

Can I scan many pages into one PDF?

Yes. Each photo becomes a page.

Can I search the text in the result?

Not by default. Run OCR PDF to add searchable text.
