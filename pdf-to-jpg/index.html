    How to use PDF to JPG

    1. Choose your PDF file.
    2. Select a resolution: 100 dpi for screens, 150 dpi for general use, 300 dpi for printing.
    3. Click Convert and download the ZIP with one JPG per page.

    Why use EasyPDF

    One image per page

    Pages are numbered page-1.jpg, page-2.jpg and so on.

    Choose the quality

    From 100 to 300 dpi, depending on whether you need small files or sharp prints.

    Deleted after conversion

    Your PDF is removed from our server as soon as the ZIP is sent.

    Good to know

    • Long documents are limited to the first 100 pages.
    • Higher resolutions make larger files and take longer.
    • Files up to 25 MB are accepted.

    Questions about PDF to JPG

    Which resolution should I choose?

    Use 150 dpi for most purposes. Choose 300 dpi if you plan to print the images, and 100 dpi for quick previews.

    Can I get a single page only?

    Use Extract pages first to pull out the page you want, then convert it to JPG.

    Why is a ZIP file downloaded?

    A PDF has many pages, so the images are packed together in one ZIP file.
