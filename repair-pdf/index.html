    How to repair a PDF

    1. Select the damaged PDF file from your device.
    2. Choose Repair PDF to send it for recovery.
    3. If recovery succeeds, download the repaired copy and open it to check the pages.

    What can PDF repair fix?

    Recovery may help with damaged cross-reference tables, malformed PDF structures or some files that fail to open. It cannot restore missing content when the source data is permanently lost.

    Privacy

    This tool processes the PDF on our server over an encrypted connection. The uploaded file and temporary conversion files are deleted after processing. See our Privacy Policy.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can every damaged PDF be repaired?

    No. Recovery depends on how much of the original PDF structure and data remains readable.

    Will repair restore deleted pages?

    No. Repair attempts to rebuild existing content; it cannot recreate data that is missing from the file.

    What is the upload limit?

    The current maximum upload size is 25 MB.
