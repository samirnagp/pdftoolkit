    Good to know

    • Animations and transitions are not kept in a PDF.
    • If a slide uses a font that is not installed on our server, a similar font is used instead.
    • Files up to 25 MB are accepted. Compress large decks with Compress PDF afterwards.

    Questions about PowerPoint to PDF

    Are speaker notes included?

    No. The PDF contains the slides only.

    Why does a slide look slightly different?

    Fonts or special effects that are not available on our server are replaced with similar ones.

    Can I make the PDF smaller?

    Yes. Use Compress PDF on the result.
