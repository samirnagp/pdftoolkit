    About Extract Pages

    Keep only the pages you need: one signature page, a chapter, or the first five pages of a long document. Extracting runs in your browser, so the file stays on your device.

    Tips for better results

    • Type ranges like 1-3 or single pages like 5 to choose what you keep.
    • Check the page numbers in your PDF viewer first.
    • Long file over 25 MB for other tools? Split it, then compress each part.

    Privacy

    This tool runs in your browser, so your file is never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

    Extract Pages: frequently asked questions

    How do I pick pages?

    Enter page numbers or ranges, such as 2, 4-6.

    Does the original file change?

    No. You get a new PDF with the selected pages.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. This tool runs in your browser.

    Can I get each page as an image?

    Use PDF to JPG on the new file.
