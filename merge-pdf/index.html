    How to use Merge PDF

    1. Choose two or more PDF files.
    2. Check the order in the list.
    3. Click Convert and download the merged PDF.

    Why use EasyPDF

    Stays on your device

    Your files are merged in your browser and never uploaded.

    Any number of files

    Limited only by your device’s memory.

    No sign-up

    No account, no watermark and no limits on use.

    Good to know

    • Files are joined in the order they are listed, usually alphabetical. Rename them 1, 2, 3 to set the order.
    • Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first with Unlock PDF.
    • Interactive elements such as bookmarks may not carry over to the merged file.

    Questions about Merge PDF

    Can I change the order of the files?

    Rename the files so their names sort in the order you want, then choose them again.

    Is there a limit on file size?

    No fixed limit. Very large files depend on your device’s memory.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Merging runs entirely in your browser.
