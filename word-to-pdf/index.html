    About Word to PDF

    A PDF looks the same on every phone and computer, which makes it the safe format for sending a resume, assignment or invoice. Upload your Word file and download a ready-to-share PDF.

    Tips for better results

    • Check the document in Word first: fix typos and page breaks before converting.
    • If the PDF is too large to email, shrink it with Compress PDF.
    • Use fonts that are common, such as Arial or Times New Roman, to avoid substitutions.

    Word to PDF: frequently asked questions

    Does it support both DOC and DOCX?

    Yes, both older DOC files and newer DOCX files.

    Why do fonts or spacing look slightly different?

    If a font is missing on our server, a similar one is used. Standard fonts convert most reliably.

    Can I send the PDF to a job portal?

    Yes. If the portal has a size limit, compress the PDF afterwards.

    Is my Word file stored?

    No. It is deleted right after the PDF is created.
