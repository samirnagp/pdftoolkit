About PowerPoint to PDF

Turn slides into a PDF so they open the same everywhere, print cleanly and are easy to email. Good for lecture notes, project decks and handouts.

Tips for better results

  • Embed or use common fonts so slide text looks as designed.
  • Animations and transitions don’t carry over, since a PDF is static.
  • Large slide decks full of photos can be heavy. Shrink them with Compress PDF.

Privacy

This tool uses our server. Your file is sent over an encrypted connection and deleted right after the result is sent. See the Privacy Policy.

PowerPoint to PDF: frequently asked questions

Do animations and videos work in the PDF?

No. The PDF shows each slide as a still page.

Are speaker notes included?

The PDF contains the slides. Notes are not added as extra pages.

Why is my PDF so large?

High-resolution photos make decks big. Compress the PDF after converting.

Which formats can I upload?

PPT and PPTX files.
