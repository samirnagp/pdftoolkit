    Why use EasyPDF

    Keeps your layout

    Deleted after conversion

    Your document is removed from our server as soon as the PDF is sent.

    Good to know

    • If your document uses a font that is not installed on our server, a similar font is used instead, so line breaks can shift slightly.
    • Files up to 25 MB are accepted.
    • Check the PDF before you send it, especially documents with unusual fonts or complex tables.

    Questions about Word to PDF

    Will the PDF look exactly like my Word file?

    In most cases yes. Documents that rely on unusual fonts may differ a little, because a similar font is substituted.

    Can I convert password-protected Word files?

    No. Remove the password in Word first, then upload the file.

    Can I protect the finished PDF with a password?

    Yes. Use Protect PDF on the result.
