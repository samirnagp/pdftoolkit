    How to use Rotate PDF

    1. Choose your PDF file.
    2. Select 90, 180 or 270 degrees.
    3. Click Convert and download the rotated PDF.

    Why use EasyPDF

    Stays on your device

    Your PDF is rotated in your browser and never uploaded.

    Three angles

    Turn pages 90° clockwise, 180° or 270°.

    No sign-up

    No account, no watermark.

    Good to know

    • The angle is applied to all pages. For only some pages, pull them out with Extract pages, rotate them, then join everything with Merge PDF.
    • A rotation of 90 turns pages clockwise, and 270 turns them counter-clockwise.

    Questions about Rotate PDF

    Can I rotate just one page?

    Not directly. Extract that page, rotate it, and merge the parts back together.

    Does rotating reduce quality?

    No. Only the page orientation changes.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Rotating runs entirely in your browser.
