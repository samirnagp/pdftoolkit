About Protect PDF

Keep a PDF private by locking it with a password. Anyone who opens the file needs the password first, which is useful for financial, medical and personal documents.

Tips for better results

  • Choose a long password that mixes words, numbers and symbols.
  • Share the password through a different channel than the file.
  • Write the password down safely. We cannot recover a forgotten one.

Privacy

This tool uses our server. Your file is sent over an encrypted connection and deleted right after the result is sent. See the Privacy Policy.

Protect PDF: frequently asked questions

What encryption is used?

AES-256, a strong standard for protecting files.

Can you recover a lost password?

No. Keep it somewhere safe.

Can I remove the password later?

Yes, with Unlock PDF and the correct password.

Is my file stored?

No. It is deleted right after the protected copy is created.
