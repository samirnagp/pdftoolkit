Protect PDF with a Password

Add a password to your PDF so only people who know it can open the file. Enter a password, and download the protected copy.

Tap to choose files or drag & drop here
    Download

    How to use Protect PDF

    1. Choose your PDF file.
    2. Type a password of 4 to 64 characters.
    3. Click Convert and download the protected PDF.

    Why use EasyPDF

    Strong encryption

    The file is encrypted with AES-256.

    Quick

    Done in seconds, and nothing to install.

    Password not stored

    The password is used only to lock your file and is not kept.

    Good to know

    • Write the password down somewhere safe. We cannot recover a forgotten password.
    • Share the password through a different channel than the file, for example by phone.
    • Files up to 25 MB are accepted.

    Questions about Protect PDF

    What if I forget the password?

    We can’t recover it, and neither can anyone else. Keep an unprotected copy somewhere safe.

    Can I remove the password later?

    Yes. Use Unlock PDF and enter the password.

    Does the password protect the file when I email it?

    Yes. Anyone who opens the file needs the password.
