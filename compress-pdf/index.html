    How to use Compress PDF

    1. Choose your PDF file.
    2. Select Smallest file, Balanced or Best quality.
    3. Click Convert and download the compressed PDF.

    Why use EasyPDF

    Three levels

    Smallest file for screens, Balanced for everyday use, Best quality for printing.

    Never bigger

    If a PDF can’t be made smaller, you receive your original file back.

    Deleted after conversion

    Your PDF is removed from our server as soon as the result is sent.

    Good to know

    • PDFs full of photos or scans shrink the most. Text-only PDFs are often small already.
    • Smaller files can look softer, so compare before you send an important document.
    • Files up to 25 MB are accepted.

    Questions about Compress PDF

    How much smaller will my PDF be?

    It depends on the content. Image-heavy PDFs can shrink a lot, while text-only PDFs may change very little.

    Will the quality drop?

    Smallest file lowers image quality the most. Best quality keeps images sharper.

    My file is over 25 MB. What can I do?

    Split it with Extract pages, compress the parts, then merge them again.
