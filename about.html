About EasyPDF

Last updated: 20 November 2026

EasyPDF is a free set of online PDF tools. Convert PDFs to and from Word, Excel, PowerPoint and JPG, merge, split, rotate, edit, compress, OCR, repair, protect and unlock PDFs, with no sign-up and no watermarks.

How it works

Merge, extract, rotate, scan, edit and images-to-PDF run inside your browser, so those files never leave your device. Other tools upload a file only for the conversion and delete it right after.

Why we built it

Forms, job portals and email limit file types and sizes. EasyPDF gets documents into the right format quickly, on desktop and mobile.

The site is supported by advertising. See the Privacy Policy.
