    How to use Excel to PDF

    1. Choose your spreadsheet (.xls, .xlsx, .ods or .csv).
    2. Click Convert.
    3. Download your PDF.

    Why use EasyPDF

    Share without editing

    Recipients see your numbers and formatting, but can’t change the cells.

    Common formats

    XLS, XLSX, ODS and CSV files are supported.

    Deleted after conversion

    Your file is removed from our server as soon as the PDF is sent.

    Good to know

    • Wide sheets can spread across several pages. In Excel, set the print area and choose to fit all columns on one page before you upload.
    • Formulas are shown as their calculated values.
    • Files up to 25 MB are accepted.

    Questions about Excel to PDF

    Why is my spreadsheet split over many pages?

    The PDF follows the sheet’s print settings. Fit the columns to one page width in Excel for a cleaner result.

    Are all sheets converted?

    Yes, the sheets in the workbook are included in the PDF.

    Can I merge the PDF with other documents?

    Yes. Use Merge PDF after conversion.
