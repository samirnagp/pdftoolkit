About Edit PDF

Fill in a form, add your signature or mark up a document without installing software. All editing runs in your browser, so sensitive papers stay on your device.

Tips for better results

  • Zoom in to place text and signatures precisely.
  • Use highlights and drawings to mark key points for a reviewer.
  • To change the original text itself, convert with PDF to Word.

Privacy

This tool runs in your browser, so your file is never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Edit PDF: frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Editing happens in your browser.

Can I add my signature?

Yes. Draw it and place it on the page.

Can I change the existing text?

This tool adds new text and marks. To rewrite existing text, convert to Word.

What can I draw or highlight?

You can add freehand drawings and highlights over any page.
