About PDF to Word

Need to change text in a PDF? Converting it to Word gives you an editable DOCX you can reword, reformat and save again. It suits contracts, reports, resumes and forms that arrived as PDFs.

Tips for better results

  • Text-based PDFs convert best. If your PDF is a scan, run OCR PDF first so there is text to convert.
  • Open the DOCX and check tables, columns and fonts. Complex layouts may need a little tidying.
  • Keep the original PDF as your reference copy.

PDF to Word: frequently asked questions

Will the formatting stay exactly the same?

It stays close for simple documents. Complex layouts, unusual fonts and multi-column pages can shift, so review the Word file.

Why is my converted Word file empty or full of images?

The PDF is probably a scan. Run OCR PDF first, then convert the result.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

Remove the password first with Unlock PDF, if you own the file.

What is the size limit?

Files up to 25 MB are accepted.
