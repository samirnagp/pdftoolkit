Extract Pages from a PDF

Keep only the pages you need. Type the pages you want, and download them as a new PDF. It runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

    How to use Extract Pages

    1. Choose your PDF file.
    2. Type the pages you want, for example 1-3,5,8-10.
    3. Click Convert and download the new PDF.

    Why use EasyPDF

    Mix single pages and ranges in one list.

    No sign-up

    Good to know

    • Separate pages with commas and use a dash for ranges: 1-3,5,8-10.
    • Pages are copied in the order you type them, and repeated numbers are used once.
    • The result is one new PDF. To split a document into several parts, run the tool once for each part.

    Questions about Extract Pages

    Can I split a PDF into many separate files at once?

    This tool makes one PDF from the pages you choose. Run it again for each additional part.

    What if I type a page that doesn’t exist?

    Pages beyond the end of the document are ignored.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Extracting pages runs entirely in your browser.
