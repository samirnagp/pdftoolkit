Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: we don’t ask you to sign up, we don’t sell data, and uploaded files are deleted right after they are converted.

1. What EasyPDF is

EasyPDF (easypdf.co.in) offers free online PDF tools. Some tools run in your browser and some use our server.

2. Your files

  • Tools that run in your browser (Images to PDF, Merge PDF, Extract pages, Rotate PDF): your files stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Tools that use our server (PDF to Word, Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF, PDF to JPG, Compress, Protect, Unlock and OCR): your file is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, processed to create your result, and deleted right after the result is sent. If something goes wrong, an automatic cleanup removes leftover files within about 15 minutes.
  • We do not read, copy, share or use the contents of your files for any other purpose, and we do not keep copies.

3. Passwords

If you type a password into Protect PDF or Unlock PDF, it is used only to perform that one operation. We do not store it.

4. Information we may collect automatically

Like most websites, our hosting providers keep technical logs, such as your IP address, the time of the request, the tool address you used, the size of the request and the response status. We use these only to keep the service running, find errors and prevent abuse. They do not contain the content of your files. Logs are kept by our hosting providers for a limited time under their own policies.

5. Cookies and analytics

EasyPDF does not use advertising cookies or tracking tools. Your browser may store the website’s own files (for the offline and installable-app features) and, if you use dark mode, nothing is sent to us about it. If we add analytics or ads in the future, we will update this page first.

6. Who helps us run the service

  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages.
  • File processing and API hosting: Render (servers may be located outside India).
  • Domain registration: GoDaddy.

These providers process technical data as described in their own privacy policies.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, file-size limits, rate limits and automatic deletion to reduce risk. No online service can promise perfect security, so please do not upload documents you are not allowed to share, and avoid sending highly sensitive files if you are unsure. For maximum privacy, use the tools that run in your browser.

8. Children

EasyPDF is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Your choices

Because we do not keep your files or accounts, there is nothing stored to delete. If you have a question about your data or a request about server logs, contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
