Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what data easypdf.co.in (“EasyPDF”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the GDPR (EU/UK), the CCPA/CPRA (California) and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Who we are

Operator: [YOUR NAME / BUSINESS NAME], [CITY, COUNTRY]. Contact: easypdf.co.in@outlook.com.

2. Your files

Images to PDF, Scan to PDF, Merge PDF, Extract Pages, Rotate PDF and Edit PDF run in your browser, and those files never leave your device. Other tools upload your file over an encrypted connection only to produce your result. Files are deleted right after conversion, and any leftover is removed within minutes. We do not read, share or sell your files, or use them to train models.

3. Data we collect

  • Technical data: IP address, browser, device, pages visited, referring page and approximate location.
  • Cookie and device identifiers set by us and by third parties for analytics and advertising.
  • Messages you send us: name, email and message content.

We have no accounts and do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used (pages viewed, time on site, traffic sources). You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

5. Advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense and possibly other ad partners. These partners, including Google, use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com, or opt out of third-party vendors at aboutads.info. See how Google uses data.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before analytics and advertising cookies are used. Change or withdraw consent any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent: analytics and advertising cookies.
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention and running the service.
  • Contract: processing the files you ask us to convert.

7. Sharing

We share data only with providers needed to run the site: our host (Render), Google (analytics and advertising) and GitHub (code hosting). We do not sell your personal information. Where data is transferred internationally we rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Retention

Files: deleted after conversion. Analytics data: [14 months]. Emails you send us: as long as needed to reply and keep records.

9. Your rights

EU/UK: access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

California: the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. Advertising cookies may count as “sharing”; opt out through “Cookie settings” or Global Privacy Control, which we honour. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

India: the right to access, correct and erase your data and to nominate someone to act for you. Grievances: easypdf.co.in@outlook.com.

To use a right, email us. We reply within 30 days (45 for CCPA requests).

10. Children

EasyPDF is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use HTTPS and limit server access. No method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
