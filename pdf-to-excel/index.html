    How to use PDF to Excel

    1. Choose your PDF file.
    2. Click Convert to Excel.
    3. Download your .xlsx file and open it in Excel or Google Sheets.

    Why use EasyPDF

    One sheet per table

    Tables are found automatically and placed on separate sheets.

    Numbers ready to use

    Amounts like 1,250.50 become real numbers, while codes such as 00123 keep their zeros.

    Deleted after conversion

    Your PDF is removed from our server as soon as the spreadsheet is sent.

    Good to know

    • Works best on PDFs made from documents or spreadsheets, where the text can be selected.
    • If no ruled tables are found, the tool splits each line of text into columns wherever there are wide gaps. Check the result.
    • Scanned PDFs have no real text. Run OCR PDF first.
    • Only the first 50 pages are converted, and files up to 25 MB are accepted.

    Questions about PDF to Excel

    Why are my columns not lined up?

    Tables without lines are worked out from the spacing. Check the sheet and adjust the columns in Excel.

    Can I convert a scanned PDF?

    Run OCR PDF first to make the text selectable, then convert the result.

    Is every page converted?

    The first 50 pages are converted. Split longer files with Extract pages.
