About Merge PDF

Combine separate PDFs, such as an application form, ID proof and certificates, into one file in the order you choose. The merge happens in your browser, so your documents are not uploaded.

Tips for better results

  • Arrange the files in the final order before you merge.
  • Rename files clearly, like 1-form.pdf and 2-id.pdf, so it’s easy to spot the order.
  • If the merged file is too big, shrink it with Compress PDF.

Privacy

This tool runs in your browser, so your file is never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Merge PDF: frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. Merging runs inside your browser.

Can I merge more than two files?

Yes, add as many as you need.

Can I reorder pages afterwards?

Use Extract Pages to pull out the pages you want in a new file.

Can I merge a password-protected PDF?

Unlock it first with Unlock PDF, if it’s your file.
