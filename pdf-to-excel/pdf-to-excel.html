About PDF to Excel

Copying a table out of a PDF by hand is slow. This tool pulls tables into an XLSX file so you can sort, filter and calculate. It works for statements, price lists and reports.

Tips for better results

  • PDFs created from software convert better than photographed pages. For scans, run OCR PDF first.
  • Check numbers and column alignment after converting, especially in tables with merged cells.
  • Simple tables with clear rows and columns give the cleanest results.

Privacy

This tool uses our server. Your file is sent over an encrypted connection and deleted right after the result is sent. See the Privacy Policy.

PDF to Excel: frequently asked questions

Will every table be converted perfectly?

Clean tables convert well. Complex or merged layouts may need small fixes in Excel.

Why are my numbers stored as text?

Some PDFs store figures as text. In Excel, convert the column to numbers.

Can I convert a scanned PDF?

Run OCR PDF first, then convert.

What is the size limit?

Files up to 25 MB are accepted.
